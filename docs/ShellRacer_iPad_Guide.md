# Shell Racer iPad guide

## Get ready

1. Open Shell Racer in Safari on the iPad. Use the dashboard menu to choose a page.
2. Put the car on the track and check that nobody is in its path. Check the car name before you take control.

## Drive with the iPad

1. Open **Drive** and choose your car from the **Car** list.
2. Press and hold **Hold to enable iPad control** until you see **iPad control** at the top.
3. Touch the **Accelerator** pad. Start near the bottom and move your finger up slowly to go faster. Lift your finger to let go of the accelerator.
4. Use another finger on the **Steering** pad. Move left or right to turn. Lift your finger to bring the steering back to the centre.
5. If you need to stop, lift your fingers and tap the red **STOP** button. Stop before changing pages or handing the iPad to someone else.
6. To hand control back to the handheld controller, let go of its trigger and tap **Return to RC**. Wait until the screen says **RC control** before driving again.

## Start a race

1. Open **Race** and set the **Lap target**. If someone is driving with the iPad, use a second device to run this page.
2. Set **Blue strips per lap** to match the track. With one strip, that strip is the start and finish line. With two strips, the car must cross two blue strips to count one lap. Ask the race organiser which setting to use.
3. Choose whether to use **Launch boost**. It gives you extra power for the first few seconds after **GO** when you press the accelerator. The usual setting is 3 seconds. Tap **Enable Race Mode**. Line up the cars. Tap **RACE** when the cars are ready, then wait for **GO** before pressing the accelerator.
4. The first car to reach the lap target wins. The other cars have up to 60 seconds to finish. If your car finishes, wait for the organiser to clear it from the track. When everyone has finished, the Race page opens Results. Look for the podium, finishing places and fastest lap. Pick an earlier race from the list to see its results. Tap Back to Race to return to the race controls. DNF means Did Not Finish. The organiser can tap **Race Finished** to end it. Use **Exit Race Mode** before normal driving again.
5. The organiser can use **Disable all cars** on the Race page to lock the fleet. If driving is locked, ask the organiser for help.

## Watch the race and hear the sounds

- Open **ShellRacer Live** to see the cars, latest and fastest lap times, and the **Race feed**. Lap times need full laps across the blue strips. An overtake may appear when the order changes at the timing line.
- For speaker sound, pair the speaker in the iPad’s Bluetooth settings. On **ShellRacer Live**, tap **Sound off** to turn sound on. Keep that page open on the speaker iPad. Use a second device to drive or run the race.
- Listen for the countdown. The music speeds up as the leader finishes laps, and the race feed shows who wins.

## Know the track colours

- **Yellow** can give a short boost. **Green** can slow the car down. **Red** is for red power. **Blue** helps count laps.
- **Fleet Settings** shows the cars’ settings. Ask the organiser before using any restore buttons. **Audio** is where the organiser manages sound clips.

## If something isn’t working

- **Car missing?** Check that the car is on. Ask the organiser to check the iPad’s Wi-Fi if the car still doesn’t appear.
- **Can’t take control?** Check that you picked the right car. If it says **iPad control elsewhere** or the fleet is locked, ask the organiser.
- **No laps?** Ask the organiser to check the blue-strip setting and detection.
- **No sound?** Check you’re on **ShellRacer Live**, sound is on, and the iPad is connected to the speaker.

## Try a simulated race

Use **Simulator** to run a practice race with virtual cars. Choose the number of cars and laps, then tap **Start simulation**. Open **ShellRacer Live** to watch the same test race. Tap **Stop simulation** to bring the real-car view back.
